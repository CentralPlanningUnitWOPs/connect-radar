The evidence

38–52%

fewer safety-related events once managers saw each event and coached.

A US government study followed drivers at two carriers. For four weeks the cameras recorded and managers saw nothing. Once managers saw each event and coached, events per 10,000 miles fell 38.1% at one carrier and 52.2% at the other. An alert puts the driver in front of your managers when Netradyne imports the day. The coaching is yours.

External research on two US carriers, quoted. It is not a Connect result. Source: FMCSA report FMCSA-RRR-10-033 (Hickman & Hanowski, 2010), abstract.

How to use it

Setting it upOff until two switches are on: one at Connect, one in your company.

  1. Connect

    Switches alert texts on for your company. Until then, alerts show in the bell only.

  2. You

    An owner adds the managers to text, each one’s UK mobile and the sites they hear about, then sets each limit.

  3. Automatic

    Each Netradyne import checks today and yesterday. A driver over a limit is flagged once, however often that day re-imports.

  4. Automatic

    Each manager gets one text per driver over a limit, and five alert texts a day at most. After that, the bell only.

Then, day to day:

  1. 1

    Open People › Netradyne Alerts. Under Who is told, pick from Add a manager…, type their mobile and press Save mobile.

  2. 2

    Under Limits, tick an event and set the number. Read Would have fired before you press Save.

  3. 3

    Press Send me a test text to see exactly what your managers will get.

  4. 4

    When a driver goes over, tap the text’s link or the bell. It opens that driver’s day on Netradyne.

  • Alert the moment it happens. It checks each time Netradyne imports, so it is as current as your last import.
  • Text drivers, or text about seatbelt, harsh driving or a blocked camera. It covers the four scorecard events, and texts managers only.
Why didn’t a manager get a text?

Texts must be switched on for your company, and the manager needs a UK mobile on the list with Text ticked. Each manager gets one text per driver over a limit, five alert texts a day at most, then the bell only. The bell still shows every one.

Will switching it on text about last week?

No. Only today and yesterday are checked, and a limit counts from the day you switch it on or change it. Re-importing a day never texts twice.
