How to use it

The cockpit, every week

Setting it upOnce: a Business Owner sets the model.

  1. Connect

    Gives your Business Owner their access, on their Owner account. Not a new login.

  2. You

    In Admin › Cockpit, set your rates, each site’s costs, and who gets each email.

  3. Automatic

    Tier, Safety and Rank are read from every scorecard you upload.

Then, every week

  1. 1

    Open Plan › Cockpit: Compare sites first, for Week to date or a finished week.

  2. 2

    Switch to Whole business for the six pillars, or open any figure for its 13-week trend.

  3. 3

    Press Send summary, preview the Owner and Manager emails, then send.

Month-end, with your accountant

Setting it upOnce: a Business Owner invites the accountant.

  1. You

    In Admin › Cockpit › Accountant, invite your accountant as Viewer or Bookkeeper.

  2. Automatic

    Amazon invoices saved in the Invoice Checker are filed as sales invoices.

Then, every month

  1. 1

    In Money › Invoices, press Upload purchase invoices. Each is read, then waits in Needs review.

  2. 2

    Check the fields and Checks, switch on Count in cockpit costs if it belongs, then Confirm.

  3. 3

    Press Export for your accountant, pick a Period, then Download ZIP: PDFs, plus Xero and QuickBooks CSVs.

It won’t
  • Count driver pay. Profit is your inflow less your own costs.
  • Reconcile, or read line items. Purchases are read from the header only.
  • Push into Xero or QuickBooks. The export is files your accountant imports.
  • Share an accountant login between businesses: it is one login per business.
What does a manager see?

The Manager version: no £ figure at all. It is built without money, not hidden. Preview it with the Manager switch.

Viewer or Bookkeeper?

A Viewer reads, downloads and exports. A Bookkeeper also uploads and confirms invoices and edits cockpit costs. Neither can change rates or settings.
