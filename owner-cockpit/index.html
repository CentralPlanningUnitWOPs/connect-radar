How to use it

Setting it upOnce: a Business Owner sets the model.

  1. Connect

    Gives your Business Owner their access. It is a grant on an Owner account, not a new login.

  2. You

    In Admin › Cockpit, set your rates, each site’s costs, and who gets each email. Business Owners only.

  3. Automatic

    Tier, Safety and Rank are read from every scorecard you upload. No scorecard, no bonus worked out.

Then, every week

  1. 1

    Open Plan › Cockpit. Compare sites comes first. Pick Week to date or a finished week.

  2. 2

    Read the profit hero and the headline tiles, then Whole business for the six pillar sections.

  3. 3

    Select a site to see the cockpit for it alone, or open any figure for its 13-week trend.

  4. 4

    Press Send summary, preview the Owner and Manager emails, then send.

  • Count driver pay. Profit is your inflow less your own costs, and driver pay is not in the model.

What does a manager see?

The Manager version: no £ figure at all. It is built without money, not hidden. Preview it with the Manager switch.

Where do the costs go?

Admin › Cockpit › Costs holds each site’s cost ledger, plus costs that belong to the whole business.
