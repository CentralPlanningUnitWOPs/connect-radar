How to use it

OnceNothing to switch on. Each email goes out in your company’s name, with the logo, colours and photos set up for your Connect.

  1. 1

    Open Today › Broadcast and pick Drivers by name, whole Sites, or Everyone at the sites you can see.

  2. 2

    Pick one of ten starters, a saved template, or write your own. Insert a driver detail adds {next_shift} and the rest.

  3. 3

    Step through drivers in Preview: it is the real email each one gets, in your brand, changing as you type.

  4. 4

    Press Send me a test for your own inbox, then Review and send to check every copy before it goes.

It won’t
  • Send by WhatsApp or text. It sends email only: one email per driver, never one group email.
  • Send a message or a list that changed after you checked it. The server checks both again and refuses.
What does each email look like?

A designed email in your brand: your logo on a header band, one of your own photos where you have one, a label and a big headline with one word in your colour, then stat tiles, fact cards, numbered steps, callouts and a button. The footer carries the driver’s own site. Ten starters come ready to edit, and Save as template keeps your own for the whole company.

Can I check it before a driver gets it?

Send me a test goes to your own sign-in address only. Review and send opens Check it, then send: how many drivers and emails, who has no email and won’t get it, and any blank detail, before anything goes. An owner can switch Broadcasts off in Admin › Emails.
