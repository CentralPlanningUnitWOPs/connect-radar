How to use it

OnceNothing to set up. Connect holds the DVLA connection, and every van with a registration is checked.

  1. 1

    Open Fleet › Van Tracker. MOT & tax sits at the foot of the Command Deck.

  2. 2

    Tap Expired, Due within 7 days or Due within 30 days to list those vans, one plain sentence each.

  3. 3

    Open a van for its DVLA record: MOT, tax and every DVLA field, with Check now to ask again.

  4. 4

    Adding a van? Type the Registration and move on: the DVLA card fills any blank Make and Year.

It won’t
  • Change a van’s status or open a VOR. A flag is information only; you decide what happens to the van.
  • Show mileage or MOT history. The DVLA gives MOT and tax status and dates, not the MOT test record.
Why does a newer van say its first MOT may already be due?

A van’s first MOT falls on its third anniversary, and the DVLA gives only the month it was first registered. So in that month Connect says it may already be due and asks you to check the V5C for the exact date.

What if the DVLA can’t find a van?

It’s counted under couldn’t be checked with the DVLA, at the foot of the panel. Tap it to see which: usually a mistyped registration. Nothing else changes.
