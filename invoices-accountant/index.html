How to use it

Setting it upOnce: a Business Owner invites the accountant.

  1. You

    In Admin › Cockpit › Accountant, invite your accountant by email as Viewer or Bookkeeper. They get an email to set a password.

  2. Automatic

    Amazon invoices you save in the Invoice Checker are filed in the vault as sales invoices.

Then, every month

  1. 1

    Open Money › Invoices and press Upload purchase invoices. Each one is read, then waits in Needs review.

  2. 2

    Check the fields and Checks, set site and category, switch on Count in cockpit costs if it belongs, then Confirm.

  3. 3

    Add Amazon’s invoices with Upload sales invoices, or let the Invoice Checker file them.

  4. 4

    Press Export for your accountant, pick a Period, then Download ZIP: the PDFs, Xero and QuickBooks CSVs and a summary.

It won’t
  • Reconcile, or read line items. The Invoice Checker still matches Amazon’s invoice to the work; purchases are read from the header only.
  • Push into Xero or QuickBooks. The export is files your accountant imports, and an accountant needs a separate login for each business.
Viewer or Bookkeeper?

A Viewer reads, downloads and exports. A Bookkeeper also uploads and confirms invoices and edits cockpit costs. Neither can change rates or settings.

Can invoices go straight to my accountant’s inbox?

If you switch on Forward purchase invoices, each one is emailed once, as it is confirmed, to their Dext, Hubdoc, AutoEntry or Xero bills inbox. It is off by default.
