Multi-cycle days, planned from one rota

Powered by

Give each site a Cycle 2 departure time, tick drivers onto it in the rota, and Day of Ops splits every van by cycle.

See it

In this demoa demo company’s departure times in Admin, a driver ticked onto Cycle 2, then Day of Ops split by cycle.

How to use it

Setting it upOnce per site: one setting, set by an owner.

  1. You

    In Admin › Dispatch Times, add the time Cycle 2 leaves and set its wave to Cycle 2. Owners only.

  2. Automatic

    The Cycle 2 column appears in that site’s rota Day view. No time set, no change.

Then, day to day

  1. 1

    Open Plan › Rota, switch to Day, and tick Cycle 2 for each driver on the second loadout.

  2. 2

    Tick Commercial to move a driver to the early wave. Their Cycle 2 tick clears, and the other way round.

  3. 3

    On the day, filter the rota by Commercial wave, Cycle 1 or Cycle 2 to see one group.

  4. 4

    Open Today › Day of Ops: vans sit under each cycle with its time and count, Cycle 2 last.

It won’t
  • Put one driver on two cycles in a day. Each driver is on exactly one: commercial, Cycle 1 or Cycle 2.
  • Send Cycle 2 a separate attendance round. Cycle 2 drivers are confirmed with the Cycle 1 round.
Does the van list image show the cycles?

Yes. Export Van List on Day of Ops bands the sheet by cycle and marks every van COM, C1 or C2.

Does it touch pay?

No. A cycle is a departure time, not a pay code, so pay and the G-Connect export are unchanged.
