The evidence

38–52%

fewer safety-related events with camera-led coaching.

A US government study followed drivers at two carriers for 17 weeks. For the first four, the cameras recorded and managers saw nothing. Once managers saw each event and coached, events per 10,000 miles fell 38.1% at one carrier and 52.2% at the other. Connect ranks the events by who to coach first. The coaching is yours.

External research on two US carriers with a different camera system, not a Connect result. Source: FMCSA report FMCSA-RRR-10-033 (Hickman & Hanowski, 2010), abstract.

How to use it

Setting it upOnce, about 10 minutes, and we do it with you.

  1. Connect

    We check your vans have their VINs in Van Tracker and your drivers have the email they use in Netradyne.

  2. You

    On a computer that stays on, press Download the Chrome extension at the foot of the Netradyne page, then add it to Chrome.

  3. You

    Press Import now in the extension and log in to Netradyne once, in the tab it opens. Your password stays with Netradyne.

  4. Automatic

    The first import brings in the last 84 days. From then on it imports every 20 minutes while Chrome is open.

Then, day to day

  1. 1

    Open People › Netradyne and start with Needs you: the worst driver, a hotspot to brief, and any driver to link.

  2. 2

    Under Where it happens, press Copy briefing: the top five spots, with map links, for the morning brief.

  3. 3

    Switch between Today, Day −1 and Lifetime for today, yesterday, or everything since the first import.

  4. 4

    In Coach first, open a driver for The evidence and The one thing to work on, then Copy image for WhatsApp.

It won’t
  • Change anything in Netradyne. It only reads, and imports pause while the computer sleeps, then catch up.
  • Email a driver by itself. Only an owner can send a snapshot, and the address is confirmed first.
What does the extension read?

Only your own Netradyne pages, as you see them. It changes nothing there, and you can revoke its key on the Netradyne page.

Why does the map show yesterday when I’m on Today?

Netradyne releases event locations once a day is finished, so today’s spots appear tomorrow morning. Everything else is live.
