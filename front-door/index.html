Indeed Automation & Booking System

Powered by

Indeed applicants get a booking link as soon as they apply, two nudges if they don’t book, and reminders once they do.

Status
Live · switch on in Admin › Recruitment Front Door
Where
People › Recruitment › Sessionsor tap Search and type “Sessions”
Who
Anyone in Recruitment. Station managers and owners run it; owners change the settings.
Before

About half our applicants booked through a Google Form. Nobody recorded who turned up.

Now

Applied, booked and attended, counted every week and by channel. Nothing is estimated.

Before

Our session times lived in four places, and moving one meant rebooking people by hand.

Now

Set a weekly pattern once and sessions roll forward. Move one and everyone booked is told.

The evidence

2.5×

the attendance when the day-before reminder says their place is booked, and good luck.

A UK Jobcentre trial texted 1,224 jobseekers the day before a recruitment event: 10.5% came after a plain message, 26.8% after one with their name, “I’ve booked you a place” and “Good luck”. Connect’s day-before email carries all three by default.

External research, not a Connect result. Source: The Behavioural Insights Team, Applying Behavioural Insights to Labour Markets (2018), p.10.

How to use it

OnceWe set up and provide the recruitment inbox and the automation that hands Indeed applications to Connect.

  1. 1

    Switch your site on in Admin › Recruitment Front Door. Applicants then tap Book Your Recruitment Session and pick a time.

  2. 2

    Open People › Recruitment › Sessions. Tick who came, then Send to Pipeline the people you want to take on.

  3. 3

    Tune the Screening questions and Messages in Admin, for the whole company or for one site.

  • Read other job boards. Only Indeed applications arrive by themselves; your other booking links take bookings, without the nudges.
  • Send by WhatsApp until your WhatsApp Business number is connected.
Which applications get a reply?

Ones whose Indeed job title contains a site’s routing words. The rest wait under Didn’t route for 30 days, so you can fix the words.

What happens when I move a session?

Everyone booked on it gets a message asking them to pick a new time, with a link to rebook.
