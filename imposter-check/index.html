The evidence

£60k

per illegal worker: the most the Home Office can fine.

Since 1 October that penalty also covers self-employed drivers. The Home Office guide says the identity step exists “to prevent impersonation and imposters”, and suggests keeping a photograph to compare later. Amazon’s notice to DSPs goes further: a repeatable imposter check, its example every 6–8 weeks, and the Amazon Delivery app’s ID check doesn’t count.

How to use it

Setting it upOff until an owner switches it on.

  1. Connect

    Ships it switched off for every company, so nothing changes until your owner chooses.

  2. You

    In Admin › Right to work, an owner switches Imposter checks on and sets the cycle: 4 to 12 weeks, 8 by default.

  3. Automatic

    Each weekday, Monday to Friday, Connect picks at random, paced so everyone is checked within the cycle. Nobody is picked twice running.

  4. Automatic

    The pick weighs only time since the last check, never nationality, visa or right to work: the same for every driver.

Then, for each pick:

  1. 1

    Open People › Imposter Check, or tap the bell. Each picked driver has a countdown ring and Start check.

  2. 2

    Press Start check on your phone with the driver there. Take a live photo, then mark each document Matches or Doesn’t match.

  3. 3

    Press Sign and complete under the declaration. Your name, the time and the method are stamped, and the record locks.

  4. 4

    Decide a failed check: Cleared, Re-check, Offboard or a Home Office report. Only Cleared or a passed re-check frees the van.

It won’t
  • Match faces for you. A manager compares the person with the documents, and an owner decides any failure: nobody is offboarded automatically.
  • Check cover drivers shift by shift, or send emails or texts. Alerts live in the bell and on the rota.
What does an auditor see?

Every check keeps who did it, when, how, the live photo, each document’s verdict and the declaration, and can’t be edited. Export CSV gives a site’s checks, or one driver’s. A driver with no photo ID on file passes on the photo and declaration alone, tagged No ID on file.

What if the picked driver is away?

An owner can press Defer this check once, moving the due date up to 14 days later, with a reason: Holiday, Sick, Not rostered / not in, or Other with a note. Who deferred it, when, why, and the original and new dates go on the record, the history and the CSV. Managers are told, and an overdue check gets its van back until the new date. A driver off site can also be checked on a live video call.
