Monthly schedules for drivers without set days

Powered by

Drivers who can’t commit to fixed weekdays declare next month on their phone. One Deploy puts them on the rota beside your set-days drivers.

Status
Live · switch on in Admin › Self-biller
Where
Plan › Capacityor tap Search and type “Capacity”
Who
Owners switch it on. Station managers move drivers across, chase and deploy. Drivers declare from an emailed link or the driver app.
Before

Some drivers can’t commit to fixed weekdays: how do they get on the rota?

Now

They tap next month’s Work and Standby days and declare. Deploy schedule puts them on.

Before

Next week, how many routes are you short until everyone has confirmed?

Now

Weekly Confirm shows confirmed shifts against routes needed, with −4 under a short day.

How to use it

Setting it upSetting it up

  1. You

    An owner ticks Offer Variable Month scheduling in Admin › Self-biller and picks the door new drivers start on.

  2. You

    On Plan › Capacity, tap a driver’s Set days chip to move them to Variable.

  3. Automatic

    On the 1st at 08:00, each Variable driver is emailed next month’s link. They have 7 days, with a reminder on day 5.

Then each month:

  1. 1

    Open Today › Weekly Confirm and the November schedule tab: drivers who haven’t declared come first, biggest gap first.

  2. 2

    Press Resend email, or Manual entry after a call. A reason is required and logged.

  3. 3

    On Plan › Rota, open Actions › Deploy schedule, choose Whole month and press Deploy.

It won’t
  • Fill in a month nobody declared. Those dates stay as they are on the rota, and the driver stays red until they declare.
  • Override a booked rest day or holiday. Deploy keeps them, whatever a driver declared.
What changes for drivers on set days?

Nothing. Deploy schedule builds them from their weekdays exactly as before, in the same press as your Variable drivers. A company that never switches Variable Month on sees one new option and no other change.

What does Weekly Confirm look like now?

It leads with next week’s capacity: confirmed route shifts against the routes needed each day, and how many short. Drivers still to confirm come first, busiest first, each with the week they’re confirming. Manual confirm is for a confirmation over the phone, stamped with the manager’s name and the time.
